    About this tool

    What it exploresExisting research related to your idea: the closest papers, what is already well covered, what we did not find, and the people to talk to.
    Who it is forResearchers and graduate students with a rough idea who want to know what has been done before they start, and whom to ask. Strongest in medicine, biology and computer science.
    How to use itType your idea and press the button. If it is too broad you get one question back: answer it and press the button again. Every paper in the report has a link.

    Sources: OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, PubMed and arXiv. Only titles and abstracts are read, never full papers, and "we did not find" does not mean "does not exist". The people suggested are authors of the closest papers found, ranked by code, not a list of the leading experts. Affiliations may be out of date.
